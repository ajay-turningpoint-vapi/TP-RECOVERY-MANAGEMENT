Clock RMS Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026 · Last updated: 24 September 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains what information the Clock RMS mobile app (the “App”) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. The App is a business tool for the recovery and collections operations of Turning Point (“we”, “us”). It is used by our salespeople, Recovery Executives, managers and administrators, and it is not intended for the general public or for children.

An account is created for you by your organisation. By signing in and using the App you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your username, name, role (for example salesperson, Recovery Executive, manager or administrator), branch and the password you use to sign in.
  • A sign-in session token, kept in your device’s secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) so you stay signed in.

Business and customer data

To do your job in the App, we show and process data from our accounting and sales systems, including:

  • Customer names, contact details, branch and assigned salesperson.
  • Invoices, outstanding and overdue balances, and payment history.
  • Records you create: call outcomes, promises to pay, follow-up tasks, disputes, payment claims, notes and escalations.

This data belongs to the business and its customers. It is handled only for collections and account-management purposes.

Content you add

  • Photos and files that you choose to attach as evidence for outcomes, disputes and physical-visit confirmations.
  • Voice dictation: if you tap the microphone on a notes or dispute-reason field, your speech is converted to text (see section 4).

Activity and device information

  • An audit trail of actions taken in the App (for example, an outcome recorded or a task completed) with the time and the user who did it.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the service, such as app version and sync status.
What we do not collect: the App does not access your location, contacts, calendar or advertising identifier, and it does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. How we use information

  • To sign you in and give you the features that match your role.
  • To manage collections: assigning customers and tasks, recording outcomes and promises to pay, tracking disputes, approvals and escalations.
  • To calculate targets, recovery scores and reports for you, your Recovery Executive and management.
  • To send notifications about tasks, deadlines and requests that need your attention.
  • To keep the App working offline and to sync your work when you are back online.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and keep an audit record.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Device permissions

The App asks for permission only when a feature needs it. You can change these in your device settings at any time.

PermissionWhy it is used
CameraTake photo evidence for outcomes, disputes and physical visits.
Photo library / filesAttach existing photos or documents as evidence.
Microphone and speech recognitionVoice dictation (including Hindi) into notes and dispute-reason fields. Speech is processed by the speech-recognition service built into your device’s operating system (Apple or Google), under their privacy terms. We do not store the audio.
NotificationsAlert you to tasks, deadlines and requests.
Network accessCommunicate with our servers.

5. Storage on your device

To support offline use, the App keeps a local copy of the data you work with in a database on your device, plus small preference settings. Your sign-in token is stored in secure storage. Signing out, or uninstalling the App, removes this local data.

6. Sharing of information

  • Within the organisation: your work is visible to the people who supervise it — for example, your Recovery Executive, managers and administrators — according to their role.
  • Service providers: we use hosting and infrastructure providers to run the servers behind the App. They process data only on our instructions.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of users.

We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

7. Security

Data is sent between the App and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are held in your device’s secure storage, and access inside the App is limited by role. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.

8. Retention

We keep business records for as long as they are needed for collections, accounting, audit and legal purposes, and then delete or anonymise them. Local data on your device is removed when you sign out or uninstall the App.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off camera, microphone, photo or notification access in your device settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal and business record-keeping requirements.
  • Ask that your account be deactivated when you leave the organisation.

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 12.

10. Children

The App is meant for employees and authorised staff and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
